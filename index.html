Studio

A private dashboard for my own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok channels.

Studio is a personal tool that runs on my own computer. It shows how my own videos perform: views, watch time, likes and audience statistics. It is not a public service and it has no other users.

When I connect one of my YouTube channels, Studio asks Google for read-only access to that channel's public data and YouTube Analytics. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.
